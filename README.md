# Chimney Sweep in Huntsville, AL

Local SEO rent-and-rank site for a chimney sweep business in Huntsville, Alabama.

## Services

- Chimney sweep
- Chimney cleaning
- Level 1 / Level 2 chimney inspection
- Chimney repair
- Chimney relining
- Chimney cap replacement

## Service areas

Serving Huntsville, Madison, Decatur, and Athens, AL, and surrounding Madison County communities.

## Website designs

Three ready-to-use designs are included in this repository:

1. **Design 1 — Classic Trust (default):** `index.html` — dark red and charcoal, trust-focused, with services, service areas, and a call-to-action.
2. **Design 2 — Modern Light:** `design-2.html` — clean light design with blue accents.
3. **Design 3 — Bold Local:** `design-3.html` — bold green and cream design with a strong local focus.

Open any of the HTML files in a browser to preview that design. `index.html` is the default homepage.
