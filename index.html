Common Questions

Questions, answered honestly

How often should a chimney be swept?

Once a year — inspection every year, and sweeping whenever creosote reaches 1/8 inch. That's the standard fire-safety guidance, even if you only burn a few fires each winter. Creosote builds faster than most Huntsville homeowners expect.

What's the difference between Level 1 and Level 2 inspection?

Level 1 checks all readily accessible parts of the chimney and flue, and it's included with every sweep. Level 2 adds a flue camera and closer checks — the one to get when buying or selling a home, after a chimney fire, or when changing fuel or appliances. Level 2 range: $234 – $562.

Do you give a fixed price before the visit?

No — and be careful with anyone who does. We publish honest ranges ($140–$374 for a sweep + Level 1), confirm the final price after inspection, and estimates are always free. There is no fixed-price guarantee.

How long does a visit take?

Most sweep + Level 1 visits take 45–90 minutes for one flue. Heavy creosote or a Level 2 camera inspection can take longer — we'll tell you before we start.

Will sweeping make a mess in my home?

No. We lay drop cloths, seal the hearth and run a vacuum throughout the visit. The soot goes into the vacuum, not onto your floors or furniture.

Which areas do you serve?

Huntsville, Madison, Decatur and Athens, AL — plus Harvest, Hampton Cove, Owens Cross Roads, Hartselle, Meridianville and Hazel Green. Each suburb has its own page in the Service Areas section above.
